STORY

Statistics degree. Operations floor. Infrastructure obsession.

Trained as a statistician — a B.Sc. and two Master’s in data — I landed not in a research lab but on the operations floor of TD Insurance in Canada. Within six months I went from contract mail clerk to operations officer; by the end I was acting team lead, and the bridge between underwriting and the automation engineers.

Somewhere between specifying the error-automation system that took completion from 45% to 90% and getting paged when a bot failed at the worst possible time, I noticed something: I cared more about keeping the system alive than about any single output it produced. Reliability wasn’t a task on my list. It was the job.

So in 2025 I made it official — moved back to Ahmedabad, went all-in on ML infrastructure, and started shipping in public. Small releases, tagged versions, automated checks, honest READMEs. I’m learning Go because the entire cloud-native stack — Docker, Kubernetes, Prometheus, Terraform — speaks it. The goal is simple: be the engineer teams trust with production ML.
